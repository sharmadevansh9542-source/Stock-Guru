Chart Patterns

Head and Shoulders

A reversal pattern signaling a trend change. It has three peaks: two shoulders and a higher head in the middle.

Bearish Reversal

Double Top

Bearish reversal pattern shaped like an "M". Indicates resistance at a price level after two peaks.

Cup and Handle

Bullish continuation pattern resembling a tea cup. The handle is a small consolidation before breakout.

Bullish Continuation

Ascending Triangle

A bullish continuation pattern with a flat resistance line and rising support line.

Descending Triangle

A bearish continuation pattern with a flat support line and descending resistance line.

Technical Indicators

Relative Strength Index (RSI)

Momentum oscillator measuring speed and change of price movements. Values range from 0 to 100.

  • Above 70: Overbought (possible sell signal)
  • Below 30: Oversold (possible buy signal)
  • Divergence may indicate trend reversal

Moving Averages (MA)

Average price over a specific period. Common types: Simple MA (SMA) and Exponential MA (EMA).

  • MA20 and MA50 are popular short and medium-term averages
  • Crossovers can signal trend changes

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

Trend-following momentum indicator showing relationship between two moving averages.

  • MACD line crossing signal line indicates buy/sell signals
  • Divergence with price can signal reversals

Stochastic Oscillator

A momentum indicator comparing closing price to recent price range, useful for identifying overbought and oversold levels.

Average True Range (ATR)

Measures market volatility by calculating the average range between high and low prices over a period.

On-Balance Volume (OBV)

Uses volume flow to predict price changes by adding volume on up days and subtracting on down days.

Parabolic SAR

Indicates potential price reversals by plotting dots above or below price bars.

Fibonacci Retracement

Identifies potential support and resistance levels based on Fibonacci ratios (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%).

Trading Techniques

Support and Resistance

Price levels where buying or selling pressure tends to pause or reverse the price direction.

  • Support: Price floor where demand increases
  • Resistance: Price ceiling where supply increases
  • Breakouts above resistance or below support can signal strong moves

Trend Analysis

Identifying the general direction of price movement to align trades with the trend.

  • Uptrend: Higher highs and higher lows
  • Downtrend: Lower highs and lower lows
  • Sideways: Range-bound price action

Volume Analysis

Volume confirms price moves. Rising volume with price increase signals strength.

  • Volume spikes often precede big price moves
  • Divergence between volume and price can warn of reversals
