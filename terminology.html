Stock Market Basics

Learn fundamental concepts of stock trading

Understanding the Stock Market

The stock market is a collection of markets where stocks (shares of ownership in businesses) are bought and sold. It serves as a platform for companies to raise capital and for investors to gain ownership in those companies.

The stock market operates through a network of exchanges, where buyers and sellers come together to trade shares. The most well-known exchanges include the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and the National Stock Exchange (NSE) in India.

Understanding the stock market is crucial for making informed investment decisions. Here are some fundamental concepts:

  • Stocks: Shares that represent a claim on a company's assets and earnings.
  • Dividends: Payments made by a corporation to its shareholders, usually as a distribution of profits.
  • Market Capitalization: The total market value of a company's outstanding shares, calculated as share price multiplied by the number of shares.
  • Bear Market: A market condition where prices are falling or are expected to fall.
  • Bull Market: A market condition where prices are rising or are expected to rise.

Key Terminology

Familiarizing yourself with stock market terminology is essential for understanding how the market operates. Here are some key terms:

  • Broker: An individual or firm that acts as an intermediary between buyers and sellers.
  • Portfolio: A collection of financial investments like stocks, bonds, commodities, etc.
  • IPO (Initial Public Offering): The process through which a private company offers shares to the public for the first time.
  • Liquidity: The ease with which an asset can be converted into cash without affecting its market price.
  • Volatility: A statistical measure of the dispersion of returns for a given security or market index.

How the Market Works

The stock market operates through a system of exchanges, where stocks are bought and sold. Here’s a simplified overview of how it works:

  1. Investors: Individuals or institutions that buy and sell stocks.
  2. Orders: Investors place orders to buy or sell stocks through brokers.
  3. Market Makers: Firms that ensure liquidity by being ready to buy or sell stocks at any time.
  4. Price Determination: Stock prices are determined by supply and demand dynamics in the market.
  5. Trading Hours: The stock market operates during specific hours, typically from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM in India.
